# Intake question bank

Pick and trim per client. Aim for about 25 questions in total, and always keep section J
(Budget). (MC) = multiple choice,
(req) = required. Block types for Tally are mapped in SKILL.md Step 2; one Tally page per
section below.

## A. About you
1. Company name and website (req)
2. What do you sell, in one sentence? (req)
3. Typical deal size (MC: <€1k, €1–10k, €10–50k, €50k+) (req)
4. Links to 3–5 of your best current customers (long text)

## B. Target companies
5. Industries to target (MC, multi-select + Other) (req)
6. Company size in employees (MC, multi-select: 1–10, 11–50, 51–200, 201–1000, 1000+) (req)
7. Countries / regions (MC, multi-select + Other) (req)
8. Must-have technologies or tools they use (short text, optional)
9. Companies or domains to exclude — current customers, competitors, partners (long text or file upload)

## C. Target people
10. Job titles or functions to reach (long text) (req)
11. Seniority (MC, multi-select: IC, Manager, Director, VP, C-level, Founder) (req)
12. How many contacts per company? (MC: 1, 2–3, 4+)

## D. Buying signals
13. Which signals mean "reach out now"? (MC, multi-select: hiring for a role, recent funding,
    posting about a topic, new tech installed, job change, event attendance, Other)
14. Keywords or topics they'd post or hire about (short text)

## E. Sources
15. Where may we look? (MC, multi-select: LinkedIn posts, LinkedIn profiles, Google Maps,
    job boards, company websites, directories, your own lists)
16. Any sources or channels that are off-limits? (long text)

## F. Volume and timing
17. Leads wanted per week (MC: <25, 25–100, 100–250, 250+) (req)
18. Start date, and is this a trial or ongoing? (short text)

## G. Destination and outreach
19. Where should leads go? (MC: Clay, Attio, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Sheet, Other) (req)
20. Who runs outreach, and with which tool? (MC: you / us; Instantly, Smartlead, Lemlist,
    HeyReach, Other)
21. Data you need on each lead (MC, multi-select: work email, phone, company data,
    personalised first line, LinkedIn URL only)

## H. Compliance (logic: show if they sell into the EU/UK)
22. Do you contact people in the EU/UK? (yes/no) (req)
23. Who owns your legitimate-interest assessment / privacy notice? (short text)
24. Do-not-contact list or suppression file? (file upload / long text)

## I. What good looks like
25. Describe a perfect lead, and a lead you'd reject (long text) (req)
26. Who approves the first batch, and how should we report? (short text)

## J. Budget (scraping costs)
Intro text: "We use Apify to find leads, and it charges per result. These answers set the
spending limits. We never go over them."
27. Monthly ceiling for scraping costs, in USD (number) (req)
28. Whose Apify account pays? (MC: yours, billed to your card; ours, passed on at cost) (req)
29. How often should new leads come in? (MC: daily, weekly, monthly) (req)
30. Maximum spend per run, in USD. We use it as a hard stop on every run. (number) (req)
31. Paid extras allowed? (MC, multi-select: full LinkedIn profiles, email finding,
    post comments/reactions, extra result pages, none)

No calculations in the form (no calculated fields, no cost totals). Evan does the maths from
the answers himself.
